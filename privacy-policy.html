Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24 August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TypeShala.

TypeShala is an online typing practice and mock-test platform. We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use TypeShala, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By using TypeShala, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, TypeShala may collect or process information such as:

Account Information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Login and authentication information
  • Account-related information

Typing and Performance Information:

  • Typing speed
  • Accuracy
  • Gross WPM
  • Net WPM
  • Test results
  • Test history
  • Typing errors
  • Weak-key information
  • Practice and performance statistics

Purchase and Access Information:

  • Purchased product
  • Transaction status
  • Amount paid
  • Purchase date
  • Pass or credit status
  • Expiry information

Payments are processed through Razorpay. TypeShala does not intend to store complete card, UPI, banking or other sensitive payment credentials on its own servers.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide typing tests and practice
  • Store and display test history
  • Calculate performance statistics
  • Provide weak-key and performance analysis
  • Manage credits and passes
  • Process and verify purchases
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect misuse or fraudulent activity
  • Maintain and improve TypeShala
  • Communicate important service information

4. Typing and Performance Data

TypeShala may retain typing-test and practice information associated with a user's account.

This may include speed, accuracy, errors, test attempts and other performance statistics.

This information is used for test results, performance history, progress tracking, weak-key analysis and targeted practice.

5. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed through Razorpay.

When making a payment, users may interact with Razorpay's payment infrastructure. Razorpay may collect and process payment information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

TypeShala generally receives transaction-related information necessary to confirm payment and provide the purchased service.

6. Third-Party Services

TypeShala uses third-party technology and service providers, including:

Supabase: Used for authentication, database and backend functionality.

Razorpay: Used for payment processing.

Vercel: Used for website hosting and deployment.

GitHub: Used for software development, source-code management and deployment-related workflows.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own policies and terms.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

TypeShala may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for:

  • Authentication
  • Maintaining sessions
  • Saving preferences
  • Application functionality
  • Improving user experience

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled through TypeShala against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, comply with applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or enforce our terms.

10. Your Rights

  • Raising privacy concerns
