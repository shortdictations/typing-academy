About Us

TypeShala is an online typing practice and mock-test platform designed to help students and competitive-examination aspirants improve their typing speed, accuracy and overall performance.

The platform is particularly useful for candidates preparing for typing and stenography-related examinations, including SSC, Stenographer, Legal/Court Typing and similar competitive examinations.

What TypeShala Offers

Depending on the services available on the platform, users may have access to:

  • Typing practice
  • Mock typing tests
  • SSC typing tests
  • Legal/Court typing tests
  • Typing speed and accuracy analysis
  • Test history
  • Performance tracking
  • Weak-key analysis
  • Targeted typing practice
  • Credits and test passes
  • Other typing-related learning features

Our Purpose

Our aim is to make typing practice simple, accessible and useful for competitive-examination preparation.

TypeShala is designed to help users understand where they are making typing mistakes and gradually improve their speed and accuracy through structured practice and performance analysis.

Continuous Improvement

TypeShala is continuously being developed and improved. New features, practice materials and improvements may be introduced from time to time.
