Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Register with your name, email, and password from the Register page. New accounts get 3 free credits automatically, usable on any SSC or Legal mock test.

What's the difference between SSC, Legal, and Combo?

SSC and Legal/Court are separate typing categories, each with their own passages and mock tests. A Combo Pass unlocks both categories with a single pass.

How is my typing speed measured?

Every test reports Gross WPM, Net WPM, and Accuracy for a fixed test duration, shown on your results and tracked on your Dashboard over time.

Mock Tests

How do I start a mock test?

Go to Start Test, choose a category and passage, then begin — tests run full-screen and timed, with your score shown immediately after.

Where can I see my past results?

My Tests (Mock History) lists every attempt you've made, with WPM, Accuracy, and date. Your Dashboard also shows your 5 most recent tests.

What unlocks a mock test?

An eligible Pass for that category, or Credits. An eligible active Pass is always used first — Credits are only spent when you don't have one, so they're never used unnecessarily.

Pass & Credits

What's the difference between a Pass and Credits?

A Pass gives unlimited tests in a category for its validity period. Credits are single-use — one credit unlocks one test attempt on any SSC or Legal test.

How long do Passes and Credits last?

Each has its own validity period, shown on your Pass & Credits page and on your Dashboard's Active Pass and Remaining Credits cards.

How do I buy more?

Visit Pass & Credits to purchase an SSC, Legal, or Combo Pass, or top up Credits — payments are processed securely through Razorpay.

Account & Profile

How do I update my name or mobile number?

Go to Settings → Account, and use the Change button next to Full Name or Mobile Number.

How do I change my password?

Go to Settings → Security → Change Password.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Go to Settings → Appearance → Theme, and choose Light, System, or Dark. Your choice is remembered across the app.
